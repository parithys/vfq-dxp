# Business Process Review — Day 12, Session 1, Recording A (Enterprise Knowledge Base Walkthrough)

**Type:** meeting notes (transcribed workshop recording with structured analysis)
**Owner / author:** not stated (vendor-produced transcript/analysis of a Vodafone Qatar workshop; document created 17 August 2026)
**Date:** Recording date 12-07-2026 11:05; duration 55 minutes

## Purpose
A guided walkthrough (by an unnamed presenter) of Vodafone Qatar's enterprise process knowledge base and several enterprise product processes, demonstrating how current as-is processes are documented and covering five specific enterprise journeys.

## Key content
- Presenter demonstrated the knowledge base structure: dashboard, process list, consumer/enterprise/internal categories, hyperlinks to drawings and step-by-step procedure documents.
- (1) Enterprise mobility lead-to-order: customer enquiry via website callback, chat, WhatsApp, business care, or business-in-retail; lead qualification by EBU operations; assignment to an account manager; document collection (CAF, MSA, and service-specific forms); credit control; discount approval in MS Dynamics via the EBU hierarchy; handover to fulfilment for account/parent account creation and number activation.
- (2) Push to Talk (PTT): same lead-to-credit-control front end, then pricing approval for device and SIM, IT and security approvals, tenant account and licence provisioning, and a service desk SR for back-end/network activation.
- (3) New fixed line / business broadband: order form, billing in advance, add-ons, RFS/feasibility checking now via Grafana on the portal, credit control, discount attached to the order form, and predefined processes covering open orders, network work and installation.
- (4) 5G Indoor and 5G Outdoor / FWA: RFS validation; the business rule that fibre takes precedence over 5G; document collection (CAF, authorised QID, Ookla speed test screenshot, customer confirmation form, terms presentation, supplementary form, employment card); CPE handover and provisioning; customer acceptance signature; post-activation support chain through business care, the CMT team, the vendor and the field partner for device faults/changes.
- (5) DAPN activation: lead, qualification, contract document collection by EBU PM and sales, order desk validation, back-end APN configuration and an SR raised in the back end.
- Recurring theme: lead creation, approvals and validation are all offline via email, with first system entry occurring only when the request reaches fulfilment; key business rules such as fibre precedence over 5G are enforced manually, not by the system.
- Action items: share the order form template; finalise and share the 5G process document; share the RFS portal views on Teams; arrange a session with the back-end teams involved in DAPN configuration.
