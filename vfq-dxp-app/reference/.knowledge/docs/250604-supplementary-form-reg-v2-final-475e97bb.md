# GigaHome & OSNtv Subscription Acceptance Form — Terms and Conditions

**Type:** contract (customer-facing subscription acceptance / terms-and-conditions form)
**Owner / author:** Vodafone Qatar P.Q.S.C (not attributed to an individual)
**Date:** not stated in body text; filename indicates 2025-06-04 ("250604") as the form's version date, version "v2_FINAL"

## Purpose
A customer-signed acceptance form for GigaHome Fiber (broadband) and OSNtv subscriptions, setting out the postpaid billing model, lock-in periods, disconnection fees, Home Move policy, Transfer of Number (TON) facility, acceptable-use rules, and eligibility conditions for a "free two months" offer, which the customer signs to confirm understanding before subscribing.

## Key content
- Postpaid billing in arrears; first bill is prorated. Fibre lock-in period is 12 months.
- New first-time GigaHome customers may get a free two-month "Try & Buy" trial. Disconnecting within the first 30 days (Try & Buy only) waives disconnection charges, but the OSN Box must be returned to a Vodafone store; if not returned or not in good working condition, penalty is QAR 165.
- Disconnection Fees if leaving before the 12-month Fibre Lock-in ends: (1) remainder of the 3-month minimum service period, (2) Fibre Lock-in charge of QR 80 x remaining months (of 12), (3) return of Giga Wi-Fi Hub if applicable, (4) OSNtv termination penalty = remainder of 12 months x QR 30 (OSN Lite) or QR 60 (OSN Premium); both Plan and OSNtv penalties apply together.
- No disconnection fee if leaving after the 12-month lock-in completes.
- Home Move is free once every 12-month cycle; must be requested 14 days before shifting. If fibre is unavailable at the new address, customer gets alternate Broadband Plans or pays Disconnection Fees.
- Transfer of Number (TON) available when moving; connection can be transferred to another customer at the same Premises.
- Vodafone may disconnect service without notice if the customer relocates/leaves the Premises; a new tenant at the same Premises may reuse the same Kahramaa ID for their own subscription.
- Connection is restricted to personal/family/roommate use at the Premises, not commercial use; Vodafone Qatar may suspend or terminate if used otherwise.
- The one-time free-months offer is tied to the customer's Kahramaa ID at the current Premises; not available again after a move to a different Kahramaa ID, or after disconnect/reconnect (same or different QID) — such behavior risks account suspension/blacklisting. Offer is one connection per household (per Kahramaa ID); multiple QID activations in the same household to re-qualify are not permitted and will result in removal of the offer.
- Form fields to be completed: Customer Name, Customer QID, Plan Name, Date, Signature.
- Company registration details stated: Vodafone Qatar P.Q.S.C, Msheireb Downtown Doha; Commercial Registration number 39656; Paid-up capital QAR 4,227,000,000.
