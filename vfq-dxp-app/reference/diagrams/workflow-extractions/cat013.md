## Journey Overview

A fixed-line (fiber) customer who is relocating requests that their existing service be moved to a new
address rather than disconnected. This is the only Home Move variant (of the fiber/5G × indoor/outdoor
matrix) that has an actual end-to-end system journey today per Doc 2; all other combinations (fiber→5G,
5G→5G, outdoor variants, etc.) are handled as manual tickets and are explicitly called out as "a pain
point." Volume is approximately 300 home moves/month across fiber and 5G combined (not fiber-only) [Doc 2].

The flow spans two systems: **Siebel/Open UI** (agent-facing order entry, feasibility check, appointment
booking) and **WFM** (Workforce Management — field technician assignment, on-site activation, inventory
transfer, service activation). The customer's old service remains live until the new location is fully
provisioned, and billing is adjusted pro-rata for the transition period [Doc 2].

## Workflow Steps (numbered)

1. **Customer initiates Home Move request.** Per Doc 2, the home move is always customer-initiated (never
   agent-initiated). Old service stays live at the current address at this point. [Doc 2]
2. **Agent enters the KID for the new/destination address and clicks Go** in Open UI/Siebel. [Doc 1]
3. **System runs an RFS feasibility check** against the entered KID and returns a pop-up: *"Location entered
   is Fiber-ready. Do you want to continue with Home Move?"* This is the feasibility decision point. [Doc 1]
4. **Agent enters the customer's existing Fixed Number and clicks Go** to pull up the account. [Doc 1]
5. **Agent enters details in the Contact Portal screen and clicks Continue.** [Doc 1]
6. **Agent verifies Address information and clicks Continue.** [Doc 1]
7. **System checks first-time-Home-Move eligibility.** If this is the customer's first Home Move, the
   customer is flagged "eligible for Free Home Move." Agent clicks Continue. [Doc 1]
8. **Agent books the field appointment:** Book Appointment screen — select Cluster, book an available slot,
   click Submit. A warning pop-up appears on submit; agent clicks Ok. System confirms "appointment booking
   successful" and displays a tabular appointment summary. [Doc 1]
9. **Agent completes the Activity Portal step:** enters activity details, including a Comments field
   (business rule: must be more than 5 characters), then clicks "Save and Finish." [Doc 1]
10. **Order is created.** A success pop-up displays the new Order ID; agent clicks Finish. System navigates
    to "My Sales Order" page in Siebel; the order shows status **Open**. [Doc 1]
11. **Field installation dependency — WFM technician workflow begins:**
    a. Field Technician / dispatcher logs into WFM and searches by Appointment ID to retrieve the activity
       detail. [Doc 1]
    b. Technician/dispatcher verifies ONT device details in UIM (checks assigned/unassigned status). [Doc 1]
    c. Dispatcher clicks "Move" to assign a technician to the job, selects the technician, and clicks Move.
       [Doc 1]
    d. Technician logs into WFM under their own user; the activity now shows as assigned. Technician clicks
       "Activate Route," then Submit, then Start (on-site). [Doc 1]
    e. Technician clicks "Get geolocation" and Submits (confirms on-site presence). [Doc 1]
12. **Technician clicks "Assess Activity."** A validation pop-up checks whether the KID is eligible for Home
    Move (a second/on-site feasibility confirmation, decision point). [Doc 1]
13. **Inventory transfer:** ONT device is transferred from Supervisor stock to the assigned technician.
    Technician verifies ONT device details again, selects the technician, clicks Move, enters ONT device
    details, and clicks "Add To Transfer List." Technician goes to the Transfer List and clicks "Start
    Transfer"; a pop-up confirms inventory transfer completed; technician clicks Ok. [Doc 1]
14. **Serial Number Check:** technician clicks "Serial Number Check," enters ONT details, and Submits.
    System returns "Service Activation Details: Success." [Doc 1]
15. **Technician completes the Home Move Checklist**, submits it. [Doc 1]
16. **Technician clicks "End," submits (with customer signature capture).** Order is marked **Complete**.
    New location is now fully provisioned/active. [Doc 1]
17. **Billing cutover:** old address service is decommissioned/deactivated and billing is adjusted pro-rata
    between old and new address service periods. [Doc 2] — exact system trigger/automation for this cutover
    step is **[UNKNOWN]**; Doc 1's happy-path walkthrough ends at technician "Order Complete" and does not
    show a distinct billing-adjustment screen.

## Decision Points

1. **RFS/Fiber Feasibility at New Address (desk check)** — triggered immediately after KID entry (Step 2).
   System responds "Location entered is Fiber-ready. Do you want to continue with Home Move?" [Doc 1]
   - **Yes (fiber-ready):** continue to Fixed Number entry / Contact Portal (Step 4 onward).
   - **No (not fiber-ready):** **[UNKNOWN]** — the happy-path document does not narrate the not-feasible
     branch (by design, it documents only the happy path). Doc 2 states that only fiber-to-fiber home moves
     have a system journey today; a not-feasible outcome at this step most plausibly routes to the manual
     ticket process Doc 2 describes for non-fiber-to-fiber combinations, but this routing is not confirmed
     by either source and is flagged as an assumption in the spec, not a hard fact.
2. **First-Time Home Move Eligibility (Free Home Move flag)** — checked after Address verification (Step
   7). [Doc 1]
   - **Yes (first-time):** customer flagged "eligible for Free Home Move," proceeds to appointment booking.
   - **No (not first-time / already used free move):** **[UNKNOWN]** — Doc 1 only narrates the flagged/free
     path; whether a repeat move is charged, blocked, or requires approval is not stated in either source.
3. **On-site KID Home-Move Eligibility (Assess Activity)** — technician-side re-validation once on site
   (Step 12). [Doc 1]
   - **Yes (eligible):** proceed to inventory transfer / serial check / completion.
   - **No (not eligible on-site):** **[UNKNOWN]** — not narrated in the happy path; presumably an exception/
     escalation path exists (e.g., re-survey, reschedule) but no source confirms mechanics.

## Exception Handling

- The Doc 1 walkthrough is explicitly the **Happy Path** only — no exception/negative branches are narrated
  in it (unlike, e.g., the FWA Indoor/Outdoor "Negative Scenarios" document that exists as a separate file
  for that journey). No equivalent Home Move negative-scenario document was found in `.knowledge/`.
- Doc 2 identifies the following as **known process gaps / exceptions handled outside the system today**,
  which are relevant context but not part of the happy-path system flow:
  - Only **fiber-to-fiber** home moves have a system journey; fiber→5G, 5G→fiber, and 5G→5G moves (and
    outdoor variants) are handled via **manual ticket**, called out as "a pain point." [Doc 2]
  - Device lock-in (12 months) commitment/lock-in **resets only on disconnection during FWA
    relocations/moves** — not on a normal device replacement; any plan change during a move resets the
    "limit date" and loses the existing lock-in terms. This is a business-rule exception relevant to
    relocation scenarios generally (stated in the FWA/home-move context of Doc 2; not confirmed
    fiber-specific). [Doc 2]
  - **[UNKNOWN]** — no source describes what happens if the WFM field appointment is missed, the technician
    finds on-site conditions blocking installation (e.g., no fiber drop, building access issues), or the
    customer needs to reschedule. This is an evidence gap, not an invented step.
  - **[UNKNOWN]** — open question flagged directly in Doc 2: "should a home move be a new order or a
    modification order" is explicitly **unresolved** in the source workshop. The Doc 1 walkthrough shows a
    new Order ID being generated at Step 10, which is consistent with (but does not definitively settle)
    the "new order" interpretation.

## Business Rules

- **Comments field validation:** the Activity Portal comments field must contain more than 5 characters
  before "Save and Finish" will proceed. [Doc 1 — verbatim captured rule]
- **Free Home Move eligibility:** a customer's *first* Home Move is flagged eligible for a free move. [Doc 1]
- **Fiber-first / feasibility gating:** the flow only proceeds past the KID-entry step when the system
  confirms the new location is "Fiber-ready"; this is the RFS feasibility gate for continuing with a
  fiber-to-fiber Home Move. [Doc 1]
- **Only fiber-to-fiber is a true system journey today**; all other technology combinations are manual
  tickets — an as-is scoping constraint on this catalogue entry. [Doc 2]
- **Customer-initiated only** — the agent does not initiate a home move on the customer's behalf. [Doc 2]
- **Continuity of service:** the old address service remains active/live until the new location is fully
  provisioned (no forced early disconnection). [Doc 2]
- **Pro-rata billing** is applied across the transition between old and new address service periods. [Doc 2]
- **Device lock-in/commitment reset:** the 12-month device lock-in commitment resets specifically on
  disconnection tied to relocation/moves; a plan change during the process resets the "limit date" and
  forfeits existing lock-in terms. [Doc 2]
- Volume/scale context (not a rule but operational sizing): ~300 home moves/month across all
  technology combinations. [Doc 2]
