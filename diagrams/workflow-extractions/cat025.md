## Journey Overview

TON transfers a mobile number (and, in a separate fixed/5G variant, a fixed-line/FWA service) from an existing customer's account to a new customer's account. It is revenue- and fraud-critical: Day4-Session2 records that the four-approval CDM design exists specifically because "a fraudster collected money from two people for the same number at different stores within an hour" — i.e. this is a hardened control, not incidental process friction. Star (premium) numbers carry additional pricing/commitment rules layered on top of the base TON flow.

Trigger: a customer (the current owner, "Party A") requests to transfer a number to a new owner ("Party B") — in store (retail), via chat, or via Digital/MVA self-service (subject to a QAR 5,000 number-value ceiling).

Actors:
- **Retail Agent** — initiates in-store TON, performs OCR/document capture, presses the "Initiate TON" button.
- **Chat Agent** — separate journey for chat-channel TON; customer scans/sends documents instead of visiting store.
- **Digital / MVA (My Vodafone App)** — self-service channel for BAU TON ≤ QAR 5,000, both parties chat/consent digitally, online payment, selfie video with ID.
- **Back-Office / Fulfilment Agent** — executes the account-transfer mechanics after approval (renames account/service account/asset, updates line items, submits order).
- **CDM team (Customer Data Management)** — reviews/validates the ticket against business rules; must approve before execution proceeds ("Tisha and [colleague]" named as CDM reviewers in the transcript).
- **Managing queue** — first of the four queues; auto-opens on ticket creation ("auto-opened since three years" per the transcript).
- **BOQ (Back Office Queue) / QATQ** — back-office queues that perform final validation and closure; routing between the two depends on the customer's nationality.
- **Fraud/Star-Number control** — business-rule layer enforcing star-number pricing/approval and blacklist checks (not a distinct human actor in the source; enforced via `ORD_VALD_RULE_070`–`073` and blacklist rules).

Three TON types (business-workshop-day7-session-1):
1. **BAU TON** — both Party A and Party B physically present; the happy path.
2. **COO / Claim of Ownership** — only the number's current user is present; the number is registered to someone else, typically due to a historical agent SIM-registration mistake. **On hold ~2 years**, retained in-system for possible future resumption — flagged here as inactive/parked, not a live path to diagram in detail.
3. **Third-party TON on a cancelled ID** — the original customer's ID was cancelled (left country/deceased); detected reactively via a Ministry of Interior (MOI) file feeding a BI-built hold-down/invalid-ID table; bulk notes uploaded monthly to Siebel Open UI; an SMS invites the eligible current user to bring the physical SIM (or eSIM screenshot) + the SMS to a store.

Automation ambition: business-workshop-day7-session-1 records a target of ~70-80% of TON cases automated, with exceptions routed to manual tickets, and TON business rules made configurable via a business-rule engine. Day4-Session2 separately records the requirement to "automate approvals via rules (SAR number, contract minimum stay) rather than human approvers," limiting human involvement to true exceptions.

## Workflow Steps

1. **Trigger — customer requests TON.** Party A (or, in third-party cases, the eligible current SIM holder) requests transfer of the number, in store, via chat, or via Digital/MVA (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:03:37], [00:15:56], [00:17:41]).
2. **TON-type determination (branch).** The process/system determines which of the three types applies: BAU (both present), COO (on hold, parked), or third-party-on-cancelled-ID (reactive MOI/BI detection route) (business-workshop-day7-session-1, [00:28:37]–[00:39:55]).
3. **TON prerequisite gate.** Before the "Initiate TON" button can be used: the number must be on prepaid **[NOTE: per business-processes-day7-session-2 [00:29:31], the as-is TON trigger requires prepaid; business-workshop-day7-session-1 separately records that migrating to prepaid pre-TON is a legacy workaround the team wants to remove — both are real, contradictory-looking statements from different sessions, kept as-is and flagged]**; Party A's QID must not be blacklisted; there must be no existing open TON ticket on the account (button is greyed out otherwise) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:07:13], [00:08:05], [00:29:31]).
4. **Pre-validation — collections/DOI check.** Neither Party A nor Party B may be in collections; this must be checked BEFORE entering the TON flow, not inside it. Entire DOI (not just first installment) and any remaining lock-in/park-my-number penalties must be cleared before transfer (business-workshop-day7-session-1, [01:12:57], [01:13:43], [01:15:17]).
5. **Age / MOI validation.** Party B's age is validated (must not be a minor) via MOI/date-of-birth, with a known 5-8 day MOI database sync-lag risk flagged for someone who just turned 18 (business-workshop-day7-session-1, [01:00:35]). Enforced in Siebel via `ORD_VALD_RULE_091`/`092` (MOI validation mandatory for Qatari ID/Diplomatic ID on New Order & TON Order; 60-minute validity window) (order-validation-business-rules).
6. **Retail agent captures system + document validations (current customer).** Split explicitly into system validations and document validations for the current customer (Party A) — signature match against QID, blacklist check, no open TON ticket (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:03:37], [00:03:48]).
7. **Retail agent captures system + document validations (new customer).** Same split for Party B: QID (or diplomatic ID) capture, OCR (unless the agent's role is permitted to bypass OCR — fulfilment/back-office roles may bypass; face-to-face store/tablet sales must go through it) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:21:22], [00:45:59]).
8. **Document collection.** Customer application form, TON form, QID/diplomatic ID for Party B (Party A may use passport/DCC/QID); for star numbers ≥ QAR 1,500, an additional customer data restoration/declaration form (listing recent usage, with a fraud-reclaim clause) is required (business-workshop-day7-session-1, [00:45:59], [01:03:51]). IDs without a signature (passports/QIDs/diplomatic IDs) require a physical thumbprint, checked by a person different from the one who entered the data — segregation of duties (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:28:14], [00:28:29]).
9. **Star-number branch — pricing/commitment check.** If the number is a star number: transfer requires EITHER a one-year postpaid commitment scaled to the number's value OR payment of the star number's value if going prepaid; commitment restarts from month one regardless of the prior customer's progress. Enforced by `ORD_VALD_RULE_070` (sold price < list price needs approver ID), `ORD_VALD_RULE_071` (sold price cannot be null), `ORD_VALD_RULE_072` (sold price cannot be negative), `ORD_VALD_RULE_073` (price must be numeric) (business-workshop-day7-session-1, [00:52:23]-[00:58:13]; order-validation-business-rules).
10. **Fee / payment step.** BAU/mutual TON carries a 50 QAR fee, collected as cash (not billed), receipt copy attached to the ticket; star-number penalty is collected manually in cash via WinCash under a separate SKU (postpaid penalty reflects automatically). Who pays the 50 QAR fee is left to store discretion today — an open gap blocking automation (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:09:01]; business-workshop-day7-session-1, [00:58:13], [01:10:53]).
11. **Prepaid-migration step (legacy, as-is only).** As-is, the line is migrated to prepaid BEFORE the TON ticket is raised, purely because back-office approval historically takes ~20 days manual validation — the team explicitly flagged this as a workaround for a validation gap, not a genuine requirement, and target-state removes it once real-time validation exists (business-workshop-day7-session-1, [00:55:16], [00:59:00]; business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:33:38]).
12. **Ticket creation and initial routing — Managing queue.** Once initiated, the TON ticket auto-opens into the **Managing queue** (auto-opened as a queue design since three years per the transcript) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:54:53], [01:05:14]).
13. **CDM queue — business-rule review/approval.** The CDM (Customer Data Management) team reviews the ticket against configured business rules; only after CDM approves does the ticket proceed to execution. This is one of the "four sequential approvals" that Day4-Session2 describes as the deliberate fraud control (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:00:09]; business-workshop-day4-session-2, [00:49:37]-[00:51:44]).
    - **Known pain point:** if the number's "actual price" is null/blank (common for old/legacy numbers with no recorded sale price, or free/star numbers with no value), the ticket is routed unnecessarily into the CDM queue even when a non-star number should not require that approval — a design gap in the queueing logic, not a product defect, and uncorrectable retroactively since the historical price data doesn't exist (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:55:14], [00:56:25], [01:01:44]).
14. **Decision — nationality-based back-office routing (BOQ vs QATQ).** After CDM validates, the ticket routes to either the **BOQ (Back Office Queue)** or **QATQ**, depending on the customer's nationality, for final validation and closure (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:55:26], [00:55:44], [01:05:14]).
15. **Back-office execution.** The back-office/fulfilment agent selects and assigns the ticket from the queue, then on the existing (Party A) customer's account and installed assets: changes the account name, service-account name, and account profile to Party B, updates the order line items so the order maps to the new customer account, and validates/submits — a single-order TON, no plan change (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:05:56]).
16. **Address association update.** New customer's address should be replicated/updated correctly since two different accounts are involved (currently agents skip this — a known gap being addressed) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:07:59]).
17. **SIM swap.** SIM swap is performed on TON success — for eSIM, an eSIM-to-eSIM swap; migrating-to-prepaid cases issue a physical SIM. Proposed (target-state) as mandatory in all cases, taking effect only upon TON success (if rejected, the old SIM remains active with the original customer) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:36:57], [00:40:47]).
18. **Order completion / closure.** The transfer completes; the TON order becomes visible **only in the new (Party B) account** — a known gap, since the old account shows no trace of what happened to the number (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:33:04], [00:41:44]).

### Fixed-line / 5G TON variant (separate sub-branch, same catalogue item per source)
19. Customer requests fixed/5G TON in-store only. Retail agent checks the same validation set (QID validity, blacklist, bill/outstanding checks) — the SIM-locking/postpaid-locking chain does not apply for fixed (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:12:19], [01:12:44]).
20. Retail agent removes the OSN add-on before raising the ticket (to avoid a discrepancy when the new customer takes it over); after account transfer, OSN/STB/other add-ons are re-added, and OSN login/email is re-registered under the new account (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:13:02], [01:14:16], [01:17:22]).
21. Spend-limit/zero-spend barring suggested during the ~3-day transition window (charging flows via OSN) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:19:38]). Bill-cycle-crossing handling: if the request crosses the bill date, the store adjusts/holds (5-day SLA) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:21:04]).

## Exception Handling

- **Rejection reasons (retail entry errors):** agent mistyping 0 as 8/9 (especially on passports), missing the last QID digit (10 vs 11 digits), signature mismatch — ~70% of signature-validation cases are affected because IDs may be 3-10 years old or PRO-signed and won't match cleanly (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:26:51], [00:27:31]).
- **IDs without a signature** (passport/QID/diplomatic without signature): fallback to physical thumbprint, checked by a second person (segregation of duties) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:28:14]).
- **POA (Power of Attorney) exception:** used when the existing customer cannot be present — under 5% of BAU cases, typically for travel/medical reasons; a POA document substitutes for direct presence (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:51:13]).
- **Null/blank number-price exception:** forces an unwarranted CDM-queue ticket for non-star numbers; flagged as a legacy data/design gap, uncorrectable for historical numbers since original sale price was never recorded (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:55:14]-[01:01:44]).
- **SIM swap rollback on rejection:** if the TON ticket is rejected, the SIM swap does not take effect and the old SIM remains active under the original customer (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:40:47]).
- **TON + Home Move conflict:** cannot be actioned as a single request — too complex; must be sequenced as separate requests (TON first, then home move) (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:23:47]).
- **Digital TON value ceiling:** self-service Digital/MVA TON is capped at number value ≤ QAR 5,000; above that the customer must visit a store (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:17:41]).
- **Segment-transfer exclusion:** Consumer↔Enterprise account transfers are explicitly out of scope for this flow — handled in a separate discussion/process (business-processes-day7-session-2, [01:26:39]).
- **Third-party-on-cancelled-ID exception path:** not proactively caught at the point of the original transaction — only surfaced reactively via the monthly MOI/BI hold-down and invalid-ID table process, with an SMS inviting the eligible user to a store (business-workshop-day7-session-1, [00:43:33]-[00:44:38]).
- **Back-office TON always requires approval:** because the OCR/CRT verification path used in retail is not available in the back-office channel, back-office-originated TON must always route through approval (business-processes-day7-session-2, [00:26:23]).

## Data Inputs / Outputs

**Inputs:**
- Party A: existing MSISDN/account, QID/passport/diplomatic ID, signature, blacklist status, collections status, outstanding DOI/lock-in balance.
- Party B: QID or diplomatic ID (free SR for QID update if needed), signature or thumbprint, age/DOB (MOI-validated), collections status.
- TON form, Customer Application Form (CAF) attachments (held across two separate accounts — A and B).
- For star numbers: customer data restoration/declaration form, sold price / approver ID (if below list price).
- Payment: 50 QAR TON fee (cash) or star-number value/commitment; migration-fee debit adjustment if applicable.
- For digital TON: selfie video (5 seconds, ID held to camera) for both parties; online payment.
- For fixed/5G TON: alternative contact number and email for the new customer's OSN/STB registration.

**Outputs:**
- Updated Siebel account: account name, service-account name, and account profile re-pointed to Party B; order line items updated.
- New SIM (or eSIM-to-eSIM swap) issued/activated for the number.
- TON order record, visible only under the new (Party B) account.
- Ticket activity trail: SMS notifications, raising agent, approving agent, CDM validation record, BOQ/QATQ closure record.
- Updated address association for the number under the new account.
- For fixed/5G: OSN/STB reprovisioned under new login/account; updated alternative contact/email in OSN and Siebel.
- Cash receipt (TON fee / star-number penalty via WinCash under a separate SKU).
