# Business Process Review — Day 7, Session 2 (Transfer of Ownership / Transfer of Number)

**Type:** meeting notes (transcribed workshop recording with structured analysis)
**Owner / author:** not stated (vendor-produced transcript/analysis of a Vodafone Qatar workshop; document created 17 August 2026)
**Date:** Recording date 05-07-2026 15:44; duration 92 minutes

## Purpose
Transcript and structured summary covering the as-is and to-be design of Transfer of Ownership / Transfer of Number (TON) for Vodafone Qatar, across retail, chat, digital, mobile and fixed/5G channels.

## Key content
- Walked through the current retail TON flow: system and document validations for existing and new customers, TON and prepaid-migration fees and their business rules, offline SMS-based communication, OCR bypass by role, POA (power of attorney)-based exceptions, and four-queue ticket routing through the Managing queue, CDM, BOQ and QATQ.
- Large part of the session was a debate on making TON real-time while the customer is in-store, evaluating options including skipping prepaid migration, suspension, and a disconnect-plus-new-activation model, weighing risks of each around number quarantine, grandfathered plans, benefit loss, rejections and fraud. Agreed controls: SIM swap on success, and mandatory approvals.
- Known pain points captured: a null/blank number price causing unnecessary CDM queue tickets (identified as a legacy data and design gap); signature and QID data-entry rejections; missing attachments; TON order visibility only available on the new account; address not being updated after TON.
- Fixed/5G TON covered separately: OSN add-on removal and re-add, login/email re-registration, spend limit control during transition, and bill cycle adjustment.
- TON solutioning was explicitly parked for a follow-up brainstorming session; FNP, MNP and disconnection topics were deferred to the next day.
